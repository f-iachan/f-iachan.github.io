Felipe S. Iachan

I study how financial, informational, and institutional frictions shape the macroeconomy and asset prices.

I am an Associate Professor of Economics and Finance at FGV EPGE, and hold a PhD in Economics from MIT (2012).

My work covers portfolio choice and asset pricing, labor markets and inequality, monetary policy transmission, credit constraints and investment, coordination in financial markets, and informality in housing and labor markets.
